Cost weights

Display

  • Total cost = materials × weight + manpower × weight + fuel × weight. Each weight goes from 1 to 10.
  • Combat power = √(attack × health). It grows with both stats and stays on the same scale as them.
  • Research year is the year the research tree gives for the tech level that unlocks a unit or building. Infantry families store this year directly. Other units unlock by level, and the year comes from that tech's year list. Forts need no research, so they have no year.
  • Building output is the base value per turn, before research bonuses and territory penalties.
  • Output gain and payback compare a building with the level it upgrades from (its req).
  • A log axis cannot show zero or missing values. The caption says how many points are hidden.
  • Upgrade lines join each unit to the next tier of its family, and each building to the level that upgrades it. The arrow points toward the upgrade.
  • Icons use the game art. Red parts take the group color, as they take the owner color on the map.
