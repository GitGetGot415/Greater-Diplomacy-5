RULE-BASED AI AND SHARED GAME RULES
=================================

AI means artificial intelligence. UI means user interface.
LLM means large language model.
The rule-based layer always runs, including when no LLM is enabled.
It generates legal actions and scores them.
Without the model, the AI uses library response text and does not hold model-based summits.

The model selects from legal actions and writes messages.
It cannot create an action that the rules forbid.
See documentation/llm_ai.txt for model settings, decisions, prompts, and request control.

This guide covers AI valuation, research, combat, and diplomacy.
See documentation/player_controls.txt for controls, player-mode permissions, reports, and map display.
See documentation/ai_movement.txt for unit orders, movement caches, aircraft AI, and movement tuning.
The shared combat rules also apply to human players.
See documentation/diplo_stuff.txt for diplomacy timing and effects.
See documentation/air_stuff.txt for the full aircraft rules.

AI MODULES
----------

map_logic/ai/ contains these shared components:

ai_world.py
    One map snapshot per turn for strength, border, and relation queries.
ai_unit_eval.py
    Unit value from its stats and the country's resource scarcity.
    Do not maintain a separate list of preferred units.
ai_tech_eval.py
    Technology value from the things it unlocks.
ai_personality.py
    Five scenario-seeded traits. Saved personalities are reproducible.
ai_opinion.py
    War, peace, trade, and alliance preference scores.
ai_candidates.py
    Legal action generation and ranking.
ai_commitments.py
    Promises and the cost of breaking them.
ai_construction.py, ai_research.py, ai_diplomacy.py
    Production, research, and proactive diplomacy.
ai_evaluation.py
    Proposal replies, messages, and proactive action selection.
ai_movement.py
    Unit orders. See documentation/ai_movement.txt.

RESEARCH INDEXES
----------------

data/queries.py indexes unit research requirements and family tiers.
Production and AI use the same unlock checks.
The active unit library supplies explicit family, tier, research, and recruitment metadata.
New research levels reuse the index without parsing unit identifiers again.
Invalidate it after library edits, metadata edits, research-year changes, gate changes, obsolescence changes, or JSON refreshes.
This includes changes made in place.

These indexes are temporary. They are not saved game fields.
AI research validates the unit index once per pass and shares it across country rankings and queue selection.
Each new pass checks content changes again. Direct queries still detect changes made in place.

AI construction submits selected purchases through map_logic/actions/production_actions.py.
Selection order, budgets, emergency behavior, site selection, and tactical-unit protection retain their existing rules.
AI building purchases retain their separate manpower policy.
Research queue construction uses the shared research owner. Its AI restart policy remains explicit.
Research selection checks the active technology prerequisites for each new project.
See documentation/general_info.txt for state and action ownership.
AI maintenance and unit orders use map_logic/actions/unit_actions.py after selection.
The diplomatic candidate collector checks shared availability before ranking or model selection.
AIWorld retains its source world's scenario settings. Evaluation reads that world's active libraries.

HISTORICAL INFANTRY EQUIPMENT
----------------------------

The Infantry category includes regular infantry, militia, cavalry, and artillery.
These research tracks include equipment stages before the original 1910 units.
Field artillery and heavy artillery use the same research track.
Earlier units use existing images through metadata.art_name.
Research labels and image requests come from level_names and level_icons in the active research library.

Read equipment dates, statistics, prices, and research costs from data/json/unit_data.json and data/json/research_template.json.
Edit data/generators/recipes.py to regenerate this content.
Unit identifiers do not determine research levels.
Explicit research metadata connects each unit to its equipment year.
Older Roman-numeral unit identifiers remain valid.
Upgrades stay within the same family and use the country's completed research.

Time-appropriate starting research supplies equipment for the selected date.
The first timeline year includes the factory research needed for army recruitment.
The special 1910 starting package includes the original infantry, militia, cavalry, and artillery equipment.
Vehicle infantry retains its separate vehicle unlocks.
See documentation/data_migrations.txt for saved research compatibility.

AIRCRAFT VALUATION
------------------

Production values aircraft as the AIR role.
Production and research use the same aircraft valuation.
Transport aircraft have a separate valuation role based on cargo capacity and range.
Ground strikes receive no air-to-air bonus.
Interception value depends on visible enemy aircraft and available air cover.
Mission estimates use stack efficiency and the execution rules in documentation/air_stuff.txt.

Owned and queued reusable aircraft reduce the need for more interception strength.
Interception-immune enemies do not create interception demand.

Unit names do not determine purchase preferences. There is no fixed fighter-to-bomber ratio.

air_pressure = hostile_air_attack / (hostile_ground_attack + hostile_air_attack + friendly_interception_attack).
Friendly interception attack includes each aircraft's shared air-to-air multiplier.
A zero denominator gives zero air pressure.
The evaluator applies interception multipliers before it subtracts enemy aircraft defense.
It does not add the separate bombardment valuation to aircraft interception or strike value.

DIPLOMACY
---------

War desire includes the power of a target's independence guarantors.
Their possible entry into the war reduces the desire to attack.

Proactive claims and declarations consider land neighbors and nearby overseas countries.
AI_NEARBY_SEA_TILES limits the connected navigable water tiles between countries.
Lakes do not count. Overseas claims target the nearby coast.
Without a shared land front, both odds calculations use overall power.
Wargoal, truce, faction, and turn restrictions still apply.

See documentation/diplo_stuff.txt for AI acceptance and conflict handling of attach?s and volunteer offers.

VALUATION RULES
---------------

Front-rank defense
    apply_group_damage uses sum(max(0, volley/N - defense)).
    N counts the legal targets in that lane's opposing front rank.
    It does not count reserve units.
    Front-rank units absorb damage through defense.
    Reserves replace losses but do not reduce the current volley.
    AI_RESERVE_DEPTH limits how many ranks the AI values.

Building research
    Value the added output of an upgrade, not the new building's total output.
    The upgrade removes the previous building level.
    A province can queue only the next building in its chain.
    Do not credit an unreachable upgrade as an immediate benefit.
    Include completed building output bonuses in upgrade values.
    Value output research from existing buildings and construction projects through the shared province output helper.
    See documentation/general_info.txt, BUILDING OUTPUT RESEARCH, for bonus data and stacking rules.

Purchase and disbanding
    Use ai_unit_eval's marginal value for both decisions.
    A separate raw-attack ranking can make the AI buy and disband the same type.

Obsolescence
    Aircraft, land units, and naval units use constants.OBSOLESCENCE_RULES.
    Obsolescence requires completed replacement research. A queued research project does not make units obsolete.
    A researched replacement removes its older family from default production and AI build candidates.
    The disband pass uses the same obsolescence query.
    Players can still choose researched older aircraft through Custom production.
    Existing units and production queues remain intact in every play mode.

TUNING
------

Shared tuning values are in data/constants.py.
Read their current values there.
See documentation/ai_movement.txt for movement-specific tuning.

AI_WAR_DESIRE_THRESHOLD
    Minimum war desire for a declaration.
AI_LINE_SPEND_RATIO
    Spending balance between unit numbers and firepower.
AI_W_SOAK
    Value of defense that absorbs damage in the front rank.
    Reserves do not absorb the current volley.
AI_RESERVE_DEPTH
    Number of ranks worth filling.
AI_TECH_ECONOMY_WEIGHT
    Research balance between industry and military needs.
AI_BUILDING_PAYBACK_TURNS
    Time allowed for a building to recover its cost.
    Keep this separate from AI_TECH_ECONOMY_HORIZON, which compares income with military value.
AI_MAX_BUILDING_RESEARCH_LEAD
    Building research levels permitted ahead of existing construction.
AI_COMMITMENT_WEIGHT
    Restraint from commitments before loyalty modifies it.
AI_MAX_ACTIONS_PER_TURN
    Limit on a country's proactive actions.
AI_UPKEEP_TARGETS
    Income share permitted for upkeep of each resource.
    Fuel limits can strongly affect army size.
    UPKEEP_MODIFIERS defines the resource upkeep rates.
COMBAT_WIDTH
    Default combat-width budget for a whole tile.
    This is a source setting, without a scenario or editor field.
COMBAT_WIDTH_BY_TERRAIN
    Terrain overrides for combat width.
    Battle controls, sidebar counts, estimates, and resolution use the same width.
    Head-on crossings use the narrower endpoint's width in either direction.
MIN_LANE_SLOTS_PER_SIDE
    Minimum allowance for a coalition side in one lane.
    share_slots divides that allowance among its countries.
    Each country present also gets at least one unit slot.
    These minimums can exceed the tile's width.
LANE_SLOTS_TYPICAL
    Default per-side slots for general purchase and research estimates.
    Movement, border strength, and enemy-front estimates use the actual terrain width.

AI PERSONALITY
--------------

Use the map editor's AI Personality button to edit and pin traits.
Pinned traits do not regenerate.
Scenario data can also set overrides:

  "ai_personality_overrides": {"German Reich": {"aggression": 0.9}}

The traits are aggression, ambition, caution, loyalty, and trust.
Each uses a value from 0.0 to 1.0.
Without an override, the scenario seed generates reproducible traits.
Generated traits need not match historical behavior.

CONSCRIPTION AND DAMAGE
----------------------

The National Economy conscription slider sets the share of manpower income kept as manpower.
The remaining share becomes materials through the existing conversion rule.
Reducing conscription also increases damage dealt by that country's units.
The bonus applies immediately to attacks, return fire, bombardment, and aircraft combat.
It multiplies the existing health and political damage modifiers.
Each unit uses its owner's conscription setting. Allies keep their own modifiers.

queries.get_conscription_damage_bonus owns the bonus calculation.
Two exponential segments meet smoothly at half conscription.
Full conscription gives no bonus. Half conscription gives one quarter of the maximum bonus.
Quarter conscription gives half the maximum bonus. Zero conscription gives the maximum bonus.
Read CONSCRIPTION_MAX_DAMAGE_BONUS in data/constants.py for the current maximum.

The dmg modifier: label shows the current bonus to the right of the conscription slider.
It includes fractional percentages and updates while you drag the slider.
Its color changes exponentially between blue, green, yellow, and red as the bonus increases.
Combat, unit panels, map forecasts, and AI battle estimates use combat_rules.effective_damage_multiplier.
Economy previews refresh when the screen opens or either slider changes.
Frame drawing reads the cached preview and damage caption.
Changing conscription also invalidates cached map forecasts and unit stats.

The existing conscription_slider field remains the only saved conscription setting.
Older saves without this field use full conscription and receive no bonus.
Tournament moves retain this setting in the player's country record.
Real-time commands validate this setting on the server before turn processing.
Tactical players cannot change national conscription. Their host country's setting still applies to their division.

LANE COMBAT
-----------

A combat side is a coalition of countries fighting together against an enemy.
Coalition members must be friendly in both directions and not at war with each other.
A contested tile has one lane for each pair of hostile sides.
Two allied countries fighting two allied enemies share one lane.
Without alliances, each hostile country pair has its own lane.

Divide terrain-adjusted combat width among the lanes.
Apply MIN_LANE_SLOTS_PER_SIDE to each side.
Every hostile country pair on the tile must share a lane.
Pairing only individual countries can split an allied battle incorrectly or omit legal opponents.

combat_rules.share_slots divides a side's slots fairly among its countries.
It repeatedly shares remaining slots among countries that still need them.
Unused slots from a small force become available to larger allied forces.
A country cannot receive less than its even share when it has enough units.
Each country present gets at least one unit slot, even if this exceeds the width.

A country fires only at countries it is personally fighting.
An ally's other wars do not create new targets.
Damage does not cross lane boundaries.
Each country fires once per turn.
A unit assigned to several lanes divides its attack among them.
It can take damage from each lane.

A country assigns a unit to every lane before adding a second unit to any lane.
If units are insufficient, it shares units across lanes instead of abandoning a fight.
Neither a lone defender nor a small attacking force can prevent another hostile side from participating.

combat_stance is a reserve preference.
Honor it only when another unit can fill the required front slot.
A country cannot avoid combat by holding every unit in reserve.
Movement clears both lane orders.

Reserves neither fire nor take ordinary lane damage.
They recover morale while front units fight.
Players can rotate units through the Battle screen.
Orders and Battle can open for any visible province.
They are read-only when the player commands no units there.
See documentation/ai_movement.txt for AI rotation, bombardment, and attack checks.

Bombardment is free and receives no return fire.
It is outside the lane system and can hit reserves.
A bombarding unit can still fight on its own tile.

DISBANDING AND FUEL
------------------

Disbanding is a last resort.
Do not disband units in combat, beside an enemy, or of a type currently being produced.
If all candidates are at the front, disband none.
Use the standing army's upkeep deficit. Do not include queued units in that deficit.

Never disband the tactical player's division.
Skip it during candidate selection so the AI can consider another unit.
ai_construction.tactical_player_unit identifies the protected division.
Protection depends on active tactical mode, not just a retained player_unit attribute.

Check fuel when purchasing.
A fuel shortage excludes fuel-consuming units from affordable candidates and saving plans.
It does not block units that need no fuel.

RESPONSE TEXT AND MESSAGE METADATA
----------------------------------

data/json/ai_responses.json stores replies for use without a model.
It uses the shared JSON cache.
A response key contains a string or a list of variant groups.
A group has interchangeable "lines" and can have a "when" condition.
The most specific eligible group wins. Select one of its lines randomly.

Supported "when" keys:
    strength: weaker, even, stronger
    at_war
    same_faction
    opinion: hostile, cold, warm

Use ai_prompts.resolve() to read a response.
AI_FALLBACK_RESPONSES remains a mapping for callers without a comparison country.
Edit the JSON to add lines.

WAR_DECLARATION stores text in the queued entry's prose field.
Its message field holds the wargoal required by queued_message.
Other actions with queued_message must also keep rule data separate from prose.

Messages also retain the action that produced them.
constants.MESSAGE_CATEGORIES maps actions to display categories:
WAR, JOIN WAR, PEACE, TRADE, FACTION, ACCESS, PUPPET, and ALLIANCE.
The protocol type remains TEXT or DIPLOMACY.
That value controls popups and the gold message bubble.

CALL_TO_ARMS and JOIN_WARS use JOIN WAR.
They combine existing wars instead of declaring a new one.
tests/test_spectator_messages.py checks that category labels fit their table column.

See documentation/llm_ai.txt for model voice rules and the llm authorship field.
