AI MOVEMENT AND UNIT ORDERS
===========================

AI means artificial intelligence. LLM means large language model.
Unit orders use rule-based AI. They do not require an LLM.
This guide covers movement planning, aircraft orders, attack checks, rotation, and temporary movement caches.
See documentation/non_llm_ai.txt for unit valuation, purchasing, disbanding, and shared combat rules.
See documentation/air_stuff.txt for the full aircraft rules.

MOVEMENT CACHES
---------------

AI movement caches candidate paths during one country's order pass.
It selects paths against current destination assignments and unit counts.
Equivalent single attackers can share first-exchange survival estimates while defenders, terrain, and diplomacy remain unchanged.
Discard these caches before the next country's pass or turn resolution.
Sharing calculations must not cause countries to share decisions.

These caches are temporary. They are not saved game fields.

DESTINATION CAPACITY AND STANDING ORDERS
----------------------------------------

ai_movement._tile_pressure prefers tiles with available front and relief capacity.
A tile with both ranks full sorts after every tile with room.
Battle attraction decreases to zero as those ranks fill.
combat_rules.nation_front_capacity calculates each country's capacity on each tile.
Keep actual unit counts separate from weighted movement scores.

DISBAND, CONVERT, and REPAIR orders survive the AI's next movement plan.

A lone guard holds a quiet peace or coastal border, in war and in peace.
The hold reads the real number of units on the tile, not the weighted assignment that carries AI_REAR_GUARD_PENALTY.

A coastal tile with no land border is a destination only when it is threatened (ai_movement._threatened_coasts).
It is threatened when, within AI_NEARBY_SEA_TILES of water from it, an enemy owns a shore or has visible units.
Lakes count. Enemies include pending war targets when surprise attacks are on.
Friendly naval supremacy removes the threat: visible friendly warship strength in that water at least
AI_COAST_SUPREMACY_RATIO times the visible enemy warship strength, and above zero.
Shores are public map data. Enemy ships count only when visible, so a hidden submarine raises no guard.
A sea surrounded by friendly land, and every coast at peace, needs no guard.

Every AI move goes through map_logic/actions/unit_actions.py, which checks each step with queries.can_unit_move_step.
A fast unit's extra steps pass the same check. A refused move leaves the unit to its next choice.

ATTACK CHECKS, BOMBARDMENT, ROTATION, AND RETREAT
-------------------------------------------------

For units with legal bombardment orders, the AI prefers an in-range bombardment over melee movement.

Before a charge, the AI estimates the first lane exchange.
It skips a hostile occupied tile if every proposed attacker would be destroyed in that exchange.
These are planning estimates. Campaign tactical encounter rules are documented in documentation/tactical_mode.md.
Shared step-by-step pinning and withdrawal rules are documented under Movement and pinning in documentation/non_llm_ai.txt.

ai_movement._rotate_damaged_units rotates damaged AI units.

A battle is lost when the shared forecast (combat_forecast.estimated_combat_outcome) says the country's side loses within AI_RETREAT_FORECAST_TURNS exchanges.
The forecast counts friendly units and visible enemy units only.
Before giving up, the AI repeats the forecast with its healthy units on adjacent tiles that could step in this turn.
If they would turn the result, the tile stays an active battle and draws reinforcements.
On a lost tile, each unit falls back to its own best neighboring tile (_retreat_tile).
That tile must be a legal step, not hostile land, free of visible enemy units, and outside unsafe waters.
A repair haven comes first, then friendly land, then the tile with the fewest visible threats nearby.
A unit with no such tile stays and fights. A convoy leaves any fight it is caught in.

A wounded unit is below AI_REPAIR_HEALTH_FRACTION of its health.
It does not charge an adjacent enemy and is not routed toward a battle or enemy land.
It can still garrison quiet tiles while it waits for a repair trip.

REPAIR TRIPS
------------

A unit repairs where it stands when it is below AI_REPAIR_ON_SITE_FRACTION on one of its country's factory tiles, out of combat, and the country can pay.
A wounded unit elsewhere makes a repair trip. It stores the destination in its repair_trip key, because the AI clears ordinary orders every turn.
Land units walk overland. Warships sail to a friendly coastal factory they may enter. Convoys do not start trips.
No trip ends on a war border or passes through an active battle.
A unit may leave a war border, or a tile next to a battle, when another friendly unit stays on the tile.
A wounded unit in a battle may leave when the country keeps enough units there to fill its current front slots (combat_rules.slots_held),
and at least one of those staying units is not wounded. The most wounded leave first.
It steps to its retreat tile and continues the trip from there.
At most AI_MAX_REPAIR_TRIP_SHARE of the country's ground and naval units, and at least one, are on trips at once.
A trip ends when the unit heals to AI_REPAIR_ON_SITE_FRACTION, when its destination stops being a haven, or when the route is cut.

AIRCRAFT ORDERS AND VISIBILITY
------------------------------

The aircraft AI generates only orders permitted by the shared rules.
Transport aircraft carry available ground reinforcements toward visible enemies through safe, accessible landing tiles.
They can load eligible ground units and drop them on reachable enemy land.
They use shared drop legality, range, and ground survival estimates against visible defenders.
Drop estimates include recovery. Recovering ground units receive no orders and deal no normal combat damage.
See documentation/air_stuff.txt for recovery duration and command restrictions.
They release cargo when no reachable safe landing tile improves its position.
Released units join the normal ground-order planning pass.
The planner preserves paid orders and the tactical player's division.
Transport aircraft receive no strike or defense candidates.
At an industry building, it upgrades numbered aircraft to the highest researched level in their current family.
It does not upgrade the tactical player's division, aircraft in combat, aircraft at threatened bases, or wounded aircraft.
An aircraft below AI_REPAIR_ON_SITE_FRACTION repairs on its country's factory base when that base is not threatened and the country can pay.
Elsewhere it flies to the nearest such factory base it can reach.
An upgrade keeps the health percentage, so a wounded aircraft repairs instead.
The AI still selects replacement missions during its normal planning pass.
It compares strikes with defense against visible enemy aircraft.
It moves reusable aircraft to closer friendly land when no enemy target is reachable and defense does not take priority.
Hostile units on friendly tiles also count as strike targets.

The planner checks which friendly provinces or troops visible enemy aircraft can reach.
It does not read enemy mission orders. Interception-immune threats do not receive patrol assignments.
It plans aircraft with larger air-to-air attack bonuses first.
Shared combat projections compare enemy equipment losses with friendly equipment losses.

Health, defense, damage modifiers, and coalition lanes use the execution rules.
Aircraft have no mission width limit. Garrison units retain terrain width.
Each coalition's aircraft use the shared stack attack efficiency.
Equipment value uses unit costs and the shared upkeep horizon.
Strike value also includes completed fort levels that a capable aircraft can remove.

Patrols are planned first. Defense value is the additional benefit after existing patrol assignments.
It counts the air exchange plus the strike damage the interception prevents.
Interception happens before a strike and only survivors strike, so the prevented damage is the threat's projected strike on the covered tile's garrison and forts,
unopposed, minus the same strike flown by the survivors.
An aircraft patrols when that benefit is positive and exceeds what it adds to the full group that could strike each of its targets.
This allows useful interceptors to defend while other aircraft strike.

Strikes are planned target-first in groups. For each reachable target, aircraft in range join strongest first,
and the group with the highest projected exchange value is kept. The best group overall flies, and the rest are planned again.
The shared stack efficiency and the target's defense decide the group size. A weak aircraft that would lower a group's damage stays out.
A strike flies only when its projected exchange value is positive.
A target with high health and strong return fire, such as a warship, can out-trade its attackers and is then left alone.
The rule reads the projected exchange, not the unit type.

The tactical player's patrol counts as cover. The planner does not change that unit's order.
The tactical player's strike counts toward its target's group.
Exchange projections are cached during one country's order pass.

Before choosing a mission, reusable aircraft leave bases threatened by visible enemy ground movement.
They choose a reachable landing tile outside that threat area.
The threat check uses current ground-unit speed and shared movement legality.
The check does not read private enemy orders or hidden units.

It avoids threatened landing tiles when moving closer to a target.
If no safe landing tile is reachable, normal mission selection continues.
Aircraft already caught in ground combat cannot escape through flight.

An aircraft with no target in range moves to a safe base nearer the enemy.
Reusable combat aircraft patrol when no worthwhile strike or closer safe base is available.
Shared capability checks select legal ground routes, patrols, or flights from current unit data.
The AI uses Convoys for reachable crossings when no land route is available.

The AI reads only visible enemy stacks.
It leaves the tactical player's division under player control.

UNASSIGNED UNITS
----------------

Normal AI unit planning remains active for units outside army groups.
The Unassigned display entry does not supply AI orders.

MOVEMENT TUNING
---------------

Read current tuning values in data/constants.py.

AI_ROTATE_HEALTH_FRACTION
    Health threshold for replacing a damaged front unit with a healthier available unit.
AI_REPAIR_HEALTH_FRACTION
    Health threshold below which a unit is wounded: it makes repair trips and does not charge.
AI_REPAIR_ON_SITE_FRACTION
    Health threshold for repairing on a factory tile the unit already stands on. A repair trip ends here.
AI_MAX_REPAIR_TRIP_SHARE
    Share of a country's ground and naval units that may be on repair trips at once.
AI_RETREAT_FORECAST_TURNS
    Exchanges the battle forecast looks ahead when deciding a battle is lost.
AI_REINFORCE_COMBAT_WEIGHT
    Attraction to a battle. It decreases as the front and relief ranks fill.
AI_REAR_GUARD_PENALTY
    Reduces movement toward a quiet border after it has a guard.
    Quiet-border guarding does not grow with combat width.
AI_COAST_SUPREMACY_RATIO
    Friendly-to-enemy warship strength at which a nearby enemy no longer makes a quiet coast need a guard.
