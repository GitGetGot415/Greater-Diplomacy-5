## Control

    Infantry and artillery related units should have a cone they have to fire from

    aim and shoot with a mouse, turn to angle the radius of this cone

    tanks can aim in a 360 degree radius, no need to angle in a certain direction

    of course, the actual fire position needs time to adjust to your mouse, so you can't immediately fire in one direction and then fire in another

## Projectiles

    Projectiles should be much faster and able to travel further (apply this to all units except artillery related ones, like artillery, railgun, railroad guns, landkeruzers)

    Artillery projectiles can look like they go in an arch, so even if a unit intersects with it it won't get hit until the shell falls back down, also the shell deals an AOE effect

## Speed

    units should have an acceleration speed, so they start slow but ease their way into moving

    this also applies to turning speed

## Visibility

    range and fire angle range should be visible for a unit you control specifically

    the arrow on your unit should stretch depending on how much you're accelerating and also turn back if you're deaccelerating to go back

    also a way to visualize how long left for your unit to reload

    hide these stats for other units, that just adds unessecary clutter to the screen

## Numbering

    1 unit per division seems rather small
    while having 1000 units per division would be more realistic, that WILL cause lag issues

    maybe something like 10 units for infantry related units

    5 units for tank and artillery related

    for a unit you control, if you die, then you stop playing for that battle, but if at least one of the units in your division survives you get to keep playing

    if your unit it at something like 70% health and you have an infantry division you start with 7 units instead of 10
    63% health? still 7 units, but they each have 90% health now
    60% health? 6 units now

    of course if you have a tank unit, which starts off with 5 units, this math is a bit different but you get the general gist
