## The three views

| Term      | Screen name       | Purpose                                       | Availability                              |
| ---       | ---               | ---                                           | ---                                       |
| Editing   | Edit Battle       | Build the armies and choose a controlled unit | Custom battles only                       |
| Preview   | Preview Battle    | Reorder your units before combat starts       | Custom battles and campaign tactical mode |
| Actual    | Actual Battle     | Fight on the battlefield                      | Custom battles and campaign tactical mode |

### Editing should only be viewable when making a custom battle.
Playing tactical mode uses preview and actual battle, with participants supplied by the campaign encounter.

Packed naval units participate in campaign tactical battles using their current Truck stats and health. Custom battle selection offers every ship type and packs selected ships through the shared ship-to-Truck conversion. Custom previews show the carried ship's icon; campaign previews show the Truck icon. On the battlefield, packed ships use the existing motorized infantry artwork, deployment, and movement behavior while retaining Truck stats. Tactical damage carries back to campaign trucks through the existing division-health fraction; ships remain packed afterward.
