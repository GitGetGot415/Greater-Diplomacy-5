## The three views

| Term      | Screen name       | Purpose                                       | Availability                              |
| ---       | ---               | ---                                           | ---                                       |
| Editing   | Edit Battle       | Build the armies and choose a controlled unit | Custom battles only                       |
| Preview   | Preview Battle    | Reorder your units before combat starts       | Custom battles and campaign tactical mode |
| Actual    | Actual Battle     | Fight on the battlefield                      | Custom battles and campaign tactical mode |

### Editing should only be viewable when making a custom battle
Playing tactical mode uses preview and actual battle
