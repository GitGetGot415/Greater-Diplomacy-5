AIRCRAFT RULES
==============

Unit data owns air_role, air_range_px, air_attack_multiplier, air_consumable,
air_interception_immune and air_damages_forts. production_group remains
presentation metadata. A missing air_damages_forts flag means no fort damage;
capabilities are read from the current unit library, including for old saves,
rather than stored on individual units or accepted from multiplayer orders.

queries.build_air_geometry indexes actual ID-map pixels as horizontal runs at
load/generation boundaries. Pixel footprints are closed unit rectangles, so
touching the radius boundary counts. Distance is from the current base's map
center to any point of the target tile, including disconnected parts; camera
zoom, tilt, viewport and horizontal presentation wrapping never change reach.
The cache is transient, rebuilt on geometry replacement, with distances cached
per base center/target. Orders previews cache mission radii at input boundaries
and draw one world-space circle per distinct base/radius, compressed by camera
tilt and closed along map-image edges wherever the circle covers an edge.
This keeps reach visible even when a small map fits entirely inside the circle,
without implying flight across the presentation seam. Rasterized outlines are
cached by base/radius/color, map dimensions, camera, viewport and clipping state.
Air targeting replaces the base's movement
outline with its strike outline; land bombardment retains tile markers. Any
part of a tile touching or inside the circle is in range. Research details
show data-driven air ranges, patrol/strike roles, damage bonuses/immunity,
consumption, interception immunity, fort damage capability, and Convoy conversion
behavior.
Command validation checks
only its target through air_target_in_range, so AI candidate validation does
not repeat full-map sweeps. Ground combat roster stats are likewise cached at
selection/state boundaries and display immediate ground casualties without
mutating the aircraft or allocating them front/reserve slots.

Patrol and reusable strikes cover the listed R, reposition reaches 2R, and one-use
strikes reach R. Aircraft fly over water, foreign land and nonadjacent provinces.
Reusable strikes spend half the one-way flight distance going out and half
returning; listed range and strike reach are the same value.
Bases and landing tiles must be land; attack targets may be water. Flight never
captures land. V1/V2 relocate along normal legal land routes at their listed
ground speed and cannot use AIR_REPOSITION or patrol. Their ranged strikes
still fly and consume the weapon. No aircraft or landed aircraft Convoy captures
territory. Aircraft Convoys use ordinary naval transport movement while loaded.
The editor unit brush likewise requires land when placing an aircraft.

AIR_PATROL orders retain base_id and priority (WEAKEST by default in old saves).
AIR_ATTACK and AIR_REPOSITION retain base_id/target_id, resolve at the next
turn boundary and clear once. Reusable strikes never remove the unit from its
launch province, guaranteeing an exact return with health damage retained.
Patrol remains active until replaced/canceled. Aircraft can only load into
Convoys, requiring a coast; unloading requires land. Conversion uses the shared
AIR_CONVERT_TURNS timer. Original aircraft stats survive via original_* transport
fields; loading refreshes former infantry placeholders preserving health ratios.
Legacy aircraft Trucks unpack on land, or become Convoys at sea, on load without
losing health fraction or identity. The heuristic AI relocates V1/V2 on land
and uses Convoys for reachable crossings when a land route is unavailable.
Old Truck conversion orders become Convoy orders on a coast and cancel inland;
normal land paths are retained for V1/V2 and discarded for reusable aircraft.

All interceptable aircraft attacking the same province form one committed
force, across nations. V2s are a separate immune force. Every hostile covering
fighter joins one shared coalition/lane engagement using global COMBAT_WIDTH,
including the ordinary three-or-more-side behavior. Missions rank by committed
calculate_unit_strength sums before losses, with target IDs breaking ties.
Patrols contribute precedence constraints between covered missions; topological
ordering preserves all compatible priorities across overlapping coverage. If
conflicting priorities create a cycle, patrols vote for their next covered
mission; most votes wins, then target ID. Surviving patrols may fight repeatedly
and lose effectiveness as health falls. A destroyed attacker never delivers a
strike.

After interception, surviving wings strike the garrison through one normal
simultaneous combat exchange, using shared coalition lanes, target-terrain
combat width, front ranks and reserves. Defenders fire back even if destroyed
in that exchange. Aircraft keep their flight stats and Tanks-category immunity;
the fighter air-to-air bonus applies only during interception. Health/political
damage modifiers apply to both sides, and fort defense protects only the
garrison. Flight never occupies or captures the target. Only bombers, Zeppelin,
V1 Flying Bomb, V2 Rocket and Jet Fighters can damage forts, controlled by
air_damages_forts in unit data. Biplane and Monoplane Fighters cannot damage
forts. Each capable wing reaching the target removes one fort level after the
exchange, including on an empty tile. Research unlock details show this capability.
Surviving reusable aircraft return to their exact base with health and morale
losses retained; destroyed aircraft are removed. A ranged barrage/strike against
a based aircraft uses the shared target damage modifiers, including
Tanks-category immunity, but never the fighter air-to-air bonus. Actual melee
ground engagement instead destroys aircraft immediately, before assembling lanes
or exchanging damage. This includes V1/V2 moving on land and aircraft unloaded
from Convoys into hostile troops. Neutral aircraft are unaffected, and
aircraft Convoys remain loaded during battles at sea.
Non-mutating UI/AI ground estimates project the same casualties. A hostile ground
presence at the launch base prevents flight from escaping the destruction rule.

Tournament aircraft use authenticated aircraft_orders commands keyed by stable
unit_id. The host validates orders against its position/type/range, retaining
its health/stats. Legacy snapshots yield order/name drafts only for existing
aircraft. New archives give each player an encrypted visibility projection and
an opaque session context, never the host snapshot's decryption secret. Legacy
archives still load. Real-time unit commands likewise validate against server
state and resolve stable IDs when filtered client indexes differ. Server guest
snapshots and initial map bundles remove hidden units and private foreign orders.
Neither projection exposes the transient geometry cache or interception lists.
