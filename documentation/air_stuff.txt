AIRCRAFT RULES
==============

AI means artificial intelligence. UI means user interface.
A wing is one aircraft unit in this guide.
A lane is a combat exchange between two hostile sides.
Each side can include several allied countries.

Unit capabilities
-----------------

The unit library owns these fields:

air_role
air_range_px
air_attack_multiplier
air_consumable
air_interception_immune
air_damages_forts

production_group is display metadata.
A missing air_damages_forts flag means the unit cannot damage forts.
Read capabilities from the current unit library, including for old saves.
Do not store them on individual units or accept them from multiplayer orders.

Aircraft research
-----------------

Aircraft levels use Roman numerals in research and production.
Research years apply the standard ahead-of-time penalty.

Each later level requires the previous level in that family.
Read current research years, levels, prerequisites, and costs in data/json/research_template.json.
Read current unit stats, capabilities, costs, and production time in data/json/unit_data.json.
data/generators/recipes.py defines data generation.
Keep its recipes consistent with data edits before you regenerate the files.
Research details derive their current information from these libraries and shared queries.
Jet Bombers use assets/classic/Jet Bomber.png.

Old saves retain their research levels and progress.
Unnumbered aircraft become level I, including transported aircraft and queued production.
Custom production choices and saved upgrade targets use the same migration.
Old scripted Spawn Unit actions create level I aircraft from unnumbered names.

Range and display caches
------------------------

queries.build_air_geometry indexes actual ID-map pixels as horizontal runs when a map loads or is generated.
Each pixel has a closed rectangular footprint.
A target touching the range boundary counts as reachable.

Measure from the current base's map center to any part of the target tile.
Include disconnected parts of that tile.
Zoom, tilt, viewport, and horizontal display wrapping do not change reach.
Flight does not cross the display seam.

The geometry cache is temporary.
Rebuild it when map geometry changes.
Cache distances by base center and target.
Command validation checks only its target through air_target_in_range.
AI candidate validation must not repeat full-map scans.

Orders caches mission radii when input changes.
Draw one world-space circle for each distinct base and radius.
Apply camera tilt to its display.
Close the outline along map edges that lie inside the circle.
This keeps reach visible when the entire map fits inside the radius.

Cache rasterized outlines by base, radius, color, map dimensions, camera, viewport, and clipping state.
Orders shows one range outline for each selected aircraft.
Aircraft with the same base, radius, and color share one outline.
Active defense and strike missions show their yellow mission range.
Active relocation shows its green movement range.
Idle reusable aircraft show green relocation range over accessible friendly land.
Hovering enemy territory or visible hostile units switches that preview to yellow strike range.
When the pointer leaves all provinces or enters a UI control, keep the previous range preview.
Hidden hostile units do not change the preview.
One-use weapons show only their yellow strike range.
Land bombardment retains tile markers.
Any tile part inside or touching the circle is in range.

Research details show library-based ranges, roles, damage bonuses, immunity, consumption, fort damage, and Convoy conversion.
Ground-combat roster stats are cached at selection and state changes.
They show immediate aircraft casualties without modifying units or assigning them front or reserve slots.

Flight and ground movement
--------------------------

queries.air_order_radius calculates mission reach from the current unit library and order type.
UI previews, AI candidates, and command validation use this shared helper.

Aircraft can fly over water, foreign land, and nonadjacent provinces.
Reusable strikes reserve travel distance for both the outgoing flight and the return.

Bases and landing tiles must be land.
Landing requires ownership, friendly relations, or military access.
Aircraft cannot land in enemy or unclaimed territory, or on hostile units.
This also restricts one-use weapons' ground routes and aircraft Convoys unloading onto land.
Attack targets can be water when hostile units are present.

Flight never captures territory.
Landed aircraft and their Convoy transports also cannot capture territory.
Loaded aircraft Convoys use normal naval transport movement.
The editor unit brush places aircraft only on land.

queries.air_unit_can_move_on_ground and queries.air_unit_can_patrol read the current unit library.
Use these checks instead of identifying capabilities by unit name.
air_consumable controls whether a completed strike consumes the aircraft.

Mission controls
----------------

AIR_PATROL retains base_id and priority.
Old saves default to WEAKEST.

The air mission button offers four choices:
- No mission.
- Defend area, weakest enemy missions first.
- Defend area, strongest enemy missions first.
- Defend area, random enemy missions first.

All choices remain visible.
Disable missions that the aircraft cannot perform.
Disable flight from an invalid launch base.

An available Defend area choice immediately issues AIR_PATROL with its selected priority.

Select aircraft and use the configured move-order click on enemy territory or visible enemy units.
The default move-order click is right-click.
That click issues AIR_ATTACK within strike range.
Hostile units on friendly territory also make the click a strike.
The aircraft attack without landing there.

The mission menu cannot issue strikes.
Clicking an active aircraft's mission button cancels its mission or relocation immediately.
No mission also cancels it.

A move-order click on accessible land without hostile units issues relocation.
The same click must not issue both strike and relocation orders.
Dispatch each map event once, including the click's release.
Land bombardment retains its existing targeting controls.

Patrol uses the mission radius from queries.air_order_radius.
Use the same tile-edge and disconnected-pixel range rules.
Cancel an active mission or relocation before giving another move-order click.
This also applies to a new strike target and a one-use weapon's ground route.
Army target-area commands also preserve active aircraft orders.
If any selected aircraft has an active mission, the movement command preserves all selected orders.
It does not extend the patrol area beyond its base range.

The menu uses No Mission, Weakest First, Strongest First, and Random First images.
An active strike uses Strike Selected.
Relocation uses Air Move, including one-use weapons with a ground path.
Idle aircraft use No Mission.
Closing the selector without a choice changes nothing.

Map boxes separate aircraft from land and naval units.
Aircraft with different missions use separate boxes, including idle aircraft and different patrol priorities.
Aircraft with matching missions can share one rectangular box.
Active missions show their command icon to the right of the box.

The icon uses the same size rule as the army emblem on the left.
Only the unit rectangle supplies selection and hover hitboxes.
Mission badges follow the map arrows' order visibility rule.
Private foreign orders and fogged stacks reveal no mission badges.

At strategic zoom, mission badges disappear and markers combine aircraft missions.
Aircraft in different armies retain separate markers.
Aircraft outside armies combine by owner and nearby position.
Land, naval, and air domains remain separate.

Choice callbacks recheck current unit identity, ownership, tactical control, and real-time planning permission.
The upgrade icon uses existing numbered-family upgrades.
Numbered aircraft can upgrade to the highest researched level in their current family.
Upgrades require an industry building and cannot start or finish in combat.
Aircraft cannot upgrade into a different family.
Mission controls add no new save fields or network command kinds.

Group missions
--------------

Set Mission shows the number of eligible selected aircraft.
It always offers all four choices.
Each choice rechecks the original units, bases, permissions, and capabilities.
With active selected missions, the button shows Cancel Mission and cancels those orders without opening the selector.
This preserves orders on selected aircraft without an active mission.

Group defense skips unsupported aircraft and preserves their orders and reserved payment.
Group strikes use the configured move-order click on a hostile target.
Each selected unit must have a legal order to that destination.
If any unit cannot reach it, the whole movement command preserves existing orders.
Group actions use existing per-unit multiplayer commands.

queries.air_unit_can_patrol determines defense eligibility from the current unit library.
Unit data defines aircraft air-to-air modifiers.

Resolution and transport
------------------------

AIR_ATTACK and AIR_REPOSITION retain base_id and target_id.
They resolve at the next turn boundary and clear once.
Reusable strikes keep the unit at its launch province.
Survivors retain damage there, which guarantees an exact return.
Patrol continues until replaced or canceled.

Saves retain RANDOM priorities. Older patrols still default to WEAKEST.
Resolution cancels saved orders that no longer meet current landing or target rules.

Aircraft can load only into Convoys.
Loading requires a coast. Unloading requires land.
Conversion uses the shared AIR_CONVERT_TURNS timer.
original_* transport fields preserve aircraft stats.
Loading replaces old infantry placeholder stats while preserving health ratios.

On load, legacy aircraft Trucks unpack on land or become Convoys at sea.
They retain identity and health fraction.
The AI uses shared capability checks to select ground routes or flight orders.
It uses Convoys for reachable crossings when no land route is available.
See documentation/ai_movement.txt for aircraft AI decisions.

Old Truck conversion orders become Convoy orders on a coast and cancel inland.
Keep normal land paths when queries.air_unit_can_move_on_ground permits them.
Discard those paths for reusable aircraft.

Interception
------------

All interceptable aircraft attacking one province form one committed force, including aircraft from different countries.
The air_interception_immune field determines which attackers form a separate force without interception.
Every hostile patrol covering the mission joins one shared coalition-lane battle.
It uses global COMBAT_WIDTH and the normal rules for three or more sides.

Rank missions by their committed calculate_unit_strength sums before losses.
Target IDs break ties for weakest and strongest priorities.
Random First shuffles each patrol's covered missions once per turn.
It does not sort missions by strength.
Patrol priorities define ordering constraints between covered missions.
Use topological ordering when those constraints agree.
If they form a cycle, patrols vote for their next covered mission.
Most votes wins, then target ID.

Surviving patrols can fight several times.
Their effectiveness decreases with health.
Destroyed attackers do not deliver strikes.

Strikes and ground combat
-------------------------

After interception, surviving aircraft make one simultaneous exchange against the garrison.
Use shared coalition lanes, target-terrain combat width, front ranks, and reserves.
Defenders return fire even if that exchange destroys them.

Aircraft keep flight stats.
queries.unit_target_damage_multiplier supplies shared target immunity and damage modifiers.
The aircraft air-to-air bonus applies only during interception.
Health and political damage modifiers apply to both sides.
Fort defense protects only the garrison.
Flight never occupies or captures the target.

air_damages_forts controls fort damage.
The turn processor applies fort damage only when the current unit library permits it.
Read the effect in map_logic/turn_processing/air_processor.py.
This also applies to empty tiles.
Research unlock details show the capability.

Surviving reusable aircraft stay at their exact base with health and morale losses retained.
Remove destroyed aircraft.

A barrage or ranged strike against based aircraft uses shared target damage modifiers.
queries.unit_target_damage_multiplier supplies these modifiers without the aircraft air-to-air bonus.

Hostile melee ground combat destroys aircraft immediately, before lane assignment or damage exchange.
This includes aircraft with ground routes and aircraft unloaded from Convoys into hostile troops.
Neutral aircraft are unaffected.
Aircraft Convoys stay loaded during sea battles.
Non-mutating UI and AI estimates show the same casualties.
Hostile ground units at the launch base prevent aircraft from escaping through flight.

Multiplayer authority and visibility
-----------------------------------

Tournament files use authenticated aircraft_orders commands keyed by stable unit_id.
The host validates position, type, and range against its own state.
It retains its own health and stats.
Legacy snapshots provide only order and name drafts for existing aircraft.

New archives give each player an encrypted visibility projection and an opaque session context.
They never provide the host snapshot's decryption secret.
Legacy archives still load.

Real-time unit commands validate against server state.
Both multiplayer systems submit final order drafts, including cancellation followed by a new movement order.
The shared player controls require cancellation before replacing an active aircraft mission with a move-order click.
Stable IDs resolve units when filtered client indexes differ.
Guest snapshots and initial map bundles remove hidden units and private foreign orders.
Neither multiplayer projection includes the temporary geometry cache or interception lists.

Last-turn reports
-----------------

Unit events records health losses and damage dealt during air combat and strikes.
Interception and strike entries show the mission tile.

Damage received identifies attackers, including aircraft at hidden bases.
Details show an attacker's base tile only when the viewer can see that unit.
Otherwise, the base tile number shows ???.
Country flags appear before identified unit names in the Details column and expanded view.
Damage dealt also identifies hidden targets, with their province number shown as ???.

Grounded aircraft destroyed by hostile troops also receive loss entries.
An expended single-use weapon receives a Weapon expended entry.
Damage dealt uses the applied combat volley, including air modifiers and target immunity.
The next completed turn removes the previous report.
