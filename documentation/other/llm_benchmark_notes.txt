HISTORICAL MODEL PERFORMANCE
============================

These notes retain earlier large language model (LLM) measurements.
They are historical observations, not current performance guarantees.
Results depend on the provider, machine, and scenario.
These notes do not specify hardware, a measurement date, or a repeatable benchmark command.
See documentation/llm_ai.txt for current prompt, budget, and fairness rules.

PROMPT SHARING
--------------

Earlier measurements used the 1941 map, 12 major countries, and llama3 through Ollama.
Shared text across phases increased from 0% to 84% after the request order changed.
Measured processing time fell from 179 seconds to 28 seconds.

TURN BUDGETS AND FAIRNESS
-------------------------

Earlier 1941-map measurements used MAJOR immersion, llama3, and one thread.
Without a time limit, all 10 directed countries and 44 model-eligible proposals were served.
About 60 calls took 8 to 10 minutes per turn.
Shorter budgets served fewer countries, with llm_last_turn rotating priority across turns.
